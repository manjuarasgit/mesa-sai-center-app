# Step-by-Step Setup Guide: Option 3 (Notion / Google Workspace Hub + WhatsApp)

This guide walks you through setting up **Option 3** for **Mesa Sathya Sai Center, AZ** completely free using Notion, Google Forms/Sheets, and WhatsApp.

---

## 1. Creating the Notion Central Wiki

1. Sign up for a free account at [Notion.so](https://www.notion.so).
2. Create a new page named: **`🌸 Mesa Sathya Sai Center — Central Hub`**.
3. Add a Cover Image (e.g. Divine Lotus or Arizona scenery) and Icon (`🌸`).
4. Set page permissions to **"Share to Web"** -> Turn on **"Allow editing"** (off) and **"Allow duplicate as template"** (off if you want read-only for public).

### Page Hierarchy to Build:

```
🌸 Mesa Sathya Sai Center — Central Hub
├── 📢 Announcements & Weekly Schedule
├── 📖 Missed Session Recaps (Database)
│   ├── Balvikas Recaps & Slide Deck Links
│   ├── Study Circle Notes
│   └── Devotional Bhajans List
├── 📦 FMSC Seva Tracker
│   ├── Event Guidelines
│   ├── Link to fmsc.org (Group Code: MESA-SAI-2026)
│   └── Embedded Google Form for Center Headcount
├── 🍲 Homeless Hot Breakfast Signups
│   └── Embedded Google Sheet (Food Checklist)
└── 📲 WhatsApp Broadcast Templates (For Coordinators)
```

---

## 2. Setting Up WhatsApp Broadcasts / Community

To reduce noise and prevent important messages from getting buried in WhatsApp chat:

1. **Create a WhatsApp Community:** Name it `Mesa Sai Center AZ`.
2. **Add Announcement Channel:** (Only Admins/Coordinators can send messages). Use this channel for weekly meeting reminders, recaps, and Seva signups.
3. **Add Sub-Groups:**
   - 👦 *Balvikas Parents Group*
   - 📦 *Seva Volunteers Group (FMSC & Breakfast)*
   - 🎵 *Bhajan Singers & Musicians Group*

---

## 3. Integrating FMSC & Hot Breakfast Signups

### For FMSC (Feed My Starving Children):
* Create a free **Google Form** or **Tally.so Form** titled *"Mesa Sai Center - FMSC Registration"*.
* Questions: Family Name, Phone, Adults Count, Kids Count, Kids' Ages, Notes, Registered on FMSC.org checkbox.
* Embed this form directly inside your Notion page under the FMSC Seva tab.

### For Homeless Hot Breakfast:
* Create a **Google Sheet** with columns: `Item Name | Quantity Needed | Volunteer Pledged | Phone`.
* Embed the Google Sheet inside Notion as an **Inline Table** or **Embed block**.

---

## 4. Weekly Workflow for Center Coordinators

1. **Sunday Afternoon (Post-Meeting):**
   - Gurus/Coordinators update the **Missed Session Recaps** page in Notion with a 2-minute summary and slide link.
2. **Monday Morning:**
   - Copy the pre-formatted WhatsApp template from the **WhatsApp Generator** page in Notion and post it to the WhatsApp Announcement Channel.
