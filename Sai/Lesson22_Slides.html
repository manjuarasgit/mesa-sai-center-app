  • Is it service if you do it to get a reward or a "thank you"?

Story Time! ✨

A Story from the SSSE Collection · India

"Meena's Choice"

A girl who wanted to serve — but had to learn how…

👂 Listen carefully — and ask yourself: Am I more like Meena at the start, or at the end?

8 / 17
🏘️ Part 1 — The Service Project

Meena was a 10-year-old girl who loved telling people about her good deeds. When her Sai center organized a food service project for poor children in the village, she jumped up immediately.

"I'll help! I'll help!" she said — making sure everyone heard.

She imagined herself handing out food, smiling for everyone to see, getting praised.

But when she arrived, the organizer looked at the list and said: "Meena — we need you in the kitchen. Chopping vegetables, washing pots. Is that okay?"

Meena's smile faded. The kitchen? Where nobody can see me?

9 / 17
🍲 Part 2 — The Invisible Work

Meena walked into the hot, steamy kitchen feeling grumpy. Her friends were outside, happily serving food. This wasn't what I signed up for, she thought.

But then an old grandmother in the kitchen noticed her and said warmly: "Come child, I'll show you how to chop. This food we make — it is prasad. We offer it to God first, then to the children."

Something changed inside Meena. She began chopping — carefully, lovingly. She washed the pots. She stirred the big pots of rice. She worked for two hours — sweaty, tired, unseen.

When the food was ready, hundreds of children received hot, nourishing meals.

10 / 17
🌟 Part 3 — The Joy No One Could See

As Meena watched the children eating happily — laughing, licking their fingers — she felt something she had never felt before. A deep, quiet joy that started in her heart and filled her whole body.

Nobody had praised her. Nobody had even seen her work. But she felt as though she was glowing from inside.

The old grandmother smiled at her: "Do you feel it, child? That feeling — that is God's way of saying thank you."

Meena went home and told her mother: "Amma, I went to serve today. But I think it was God who served ME." 💛

11 / 17
💡
What Does the Story Teach Us?
  • True seva is done without expectation of praise or reward
  • It doesn't matter if the job is big or small, glamorous or unglamorous — do it with love 💛
  • Service done with a pure heart is offered to God — it is prasad! 🙏
  • The joy you feel inside after real seva — that is God's thanks to you
  • We don't do service to get something — we do it because we already have love ❤️
"Service to man is service to God."
— Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba

The Right Way to Serve
✅ DO this:
  • Serve with a smile and a willing heart
  • Treat everyone you serve with dignity
  • Offer your service to God first
  • Do the small, unseen jobs too
  • Feel grateful for the chance to serve
❌ Avoid this:
  • Serving only when people are watching
  • Expecting praise or reward
  • Feeling superior to those you help
  • Doing service with ego or pride
  • Complaining or doing it half-heartedly

🌿 The secret ingredient? LOVE. That's what turns ordinary work into Seva!
