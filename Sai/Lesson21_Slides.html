Story Time! ✨

A Story from the SSSE Collection · India

"Where Does God Live?"

A curious boy kept asking one question that nobody could answer…

👂 Listen carefully — the answer may surprise you!

8 / 16
🏫 Part 1 — The Question

Raju was a boy in a small school in India. Every single day, he asked his teacher the same question:

"Teacher, WHERE does God live? I want to see God!"

The teacher smiled and said: "Alright, Raju. Let's go find God together — right now!"

Raju was surprised. "We can find God? Right here? Today?"

The teacher nodded. "Put on your shoes. We are going for a walk."

9 / 16
🚶 Part 2 — The Search

They walked through the school. The teacher stopped at a student helping a friend with math homework.

"Did you see God, Raju?" — "No, Teacher! I only saw students."

They walked past a worker carefully sweeping the courtyard with a wide smile.

"Did you see God there?" — "No, Teacher! I only saw a sweeper."

They passed the kitchen, where a kind lady was serving lunch, placing food on each plate with love.

"Did you see God there?" — "No, Teacher! I only saw the cook!"

10 / 16
🌟 Part 3 — The Answer!

The teacher sat down with Raju and said gently:

"Look again, my child."

"The student who helped his friend — God was in those hands. The worker who swept with a smile — God was in that joy. The cook who served with love — God was in that kindness."

Raju's eyes went wide: "So… God is not far away?"

The teacher smiled: "God is right here — in every act of love, in every kind heart, in every helping hand. God is in me. God is in you. God is everywhere, always."

That day, Raju finally saw God — and he could not stop smiling. 😊

11 / 16
💡
What does the Story Teach Us?
  • God doesn't live only in a temple — God lives in every person
  • Every act of love, kindness, and service is God showing up
  • God visits us every day — in friends, family, teachers, and strangers 🙏
  • We don't need to look for God — we need to feel God with our heart ❤️
"God is in every name and every form."
— Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba

The Language of the Heart
People across the world speak thousands of languages.
But God speaks just ONE universal language —

❤️ THE LANGUAGE OF LOVE ❤️

  • A smile of kindness needs no translation 😊
  • A helping hand is understood everywhere 🤝
  • A loving heart finds God in everyone it meets 🌍

🌈 When we speak the language of love — we speak directly to God!
